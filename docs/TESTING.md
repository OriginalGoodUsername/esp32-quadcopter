# Build observations

My observations from the completed esp-fc quadcopter build:

- F310 inputs registered in the receiver monitor, including roll, pitch, throttle, yaw and arm/disarm.
- All four ESCs were calibrated, and motor mapping and spin direction were checked.
- The quadcopter took off, hovered and flew outdoors under manual gamepad control with self-level stabilization.
- Cutting the wireless control link stopped all four motors.
